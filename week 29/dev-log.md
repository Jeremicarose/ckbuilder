## Product Validation Direction

The main validation question remains:

> Does a persistent, owner-controlled, cross-network receiver identity provide enough practical value that receiver operators would actually use or integrate it?

The pilot is being structured around real operator workflows, including:

- receiver identity
- ownership
- relocation
- replacement
- multi-network feeding
- lifecycle history
- revocation
- operational status
- observation provenance
- MLAT integration

The goal is to determine whether the Registry solves a meaningful problem before expanding toward broader adoption.

## Productisation

Based on feedback that external recruitment is currently the main bottleneck, the project is being packaged more clearly as a structured pilot programme.

The proposed programme will define:

- purpose and value for operators
- participation requirements
- testing process
- expected time commitment
- privacy and data boundaries
- technical requirements
- potential tester compensation
- feedback and validation criteria

A potential CKB community-funded paid tester programme is also being explored.
